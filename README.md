# Back to nature+

**A mod for Harvest Moon: Back to Nature (PS1, USA)**

Back to nature+ keeps the game you know and adds QOL improvements, a stronger tool tier, farm upgrades that automate tedious actions, coloured hens, new outfits, a new berry and faster boots. Everything uses the game's own art and style. I haven't added any new art into the game all changes were made using the original assets.

## New in v1.6

### Town horse
- **Options > Town horse.** Off by default. A permanent choice for that save.
- Ride your horse off the farm. Get off anywhere and he waits for you there.
- At the beach you get off at the town square and he waits for you there. 
- If a scene takes you indoors while riding, you get off and he goes home to the stable. Left in town overnight, he's back in the stable by morning.
- Throwing things into his saddlebag off the farm ships them as well. 

### Tack colors
- Barley sells 12 saddle colours (the pony wears them as a scarf) once you own the pony or horse. 500G each, buy once and switch for free any time. The original is always free.
- The colour shows in cutscenes and the horse race too.

### Golden brush
- Bring Saibara your brush and a gold ore to upgrade the brush. Brushing with it counts double for cows, sheep and the horse. It costs 3000G and 3 days like Saibara's other jobs. 

### Shuffle field and All seeds
- **Options > Shuffle field** (first day only, top of the list): opens its own page. Shuffle the weeds, stones, branches, stumps and rocks on your field as often as you like.
- **Options > All seeds** (one-time choice, Classic or All seeds): Jeff sells strawberry, pumpkin and spinach from day one. Once you choose, the row leaves the menu.

### Clock settings
- **Options > Clock settings**, change them any time:
  - **Speed: Normal, Half or Quarter.** Half makes each day last twice as long, Quarter four times. A new speed starts the next morning. Villagers keep to their schedules at every speed.
  - **Minutes: On or Off.** Off by default. The clock shows every minute like PM 8:47

### Wild weather
- **Options > Wild weather**, a new page, its switches are permanent for that save.
- **Rainy mineral town:** the odds for sunny days get flipped to rainy days (not in winter). Once it's on, the row leaves the menu.

### Easy seasons
- **Options > Easy seasons**, a new page next to Hard seasons. Its switches are permanent for that save.
- **No hurricanes:** hurricanes come as rain and blizzards as snow.

### Classic game
- **Options > Classic game.** Off by default. A permanent choice for that save.
- Play the original game without the mod's new content: no Gotz upgrades, no Adamantite tier, no golden brush, no new outfits, no running boots, no miracle berry, no tack colours, no ocarina, no coloured hens, and no extra gold-badge animals at the festivals.
- All your options and every fix stay. Classic game only takes out the new content.
- Only offered on a save that hasn't bought or earned any of the extras yet, works best on a new game. Nothing you own is taken away.

### Fixes
- The Cooking Festival's result fanfare played the ocarina's tune since v1.4. It plays its own fanfare again.
- The Miracle berry now also stops the stray dog's loss while you sleep with it still on the farm.


## New in v1.5

### Marry all
- **Options > Marry all.** Off by default. Once you turn it on it stays on for that save and leaves the options menu. The setting asks you first.
- Marry every girl in town. Your first wife lives with you on the farm and the others stay at home in town.
- The Blue Feather goes back on the shelf after each wedding, one feather per girl. Each wedding gets its own date and its own heart on the calendar.
- Every wife talks to you as your wife, everywhere. Their families treat you as married. 
- Wives you married can be found on the Villagers page with a small blue feather next to their name.
- The rivals still compete. A girl you haven't married can still marry her rival, just like the original game.
- A girl's own heart scenes still play while you're married to someone else.
- **Perk:** a girl you've married lets you into her house and her work any time of day, this includes festivals. Karens is the best one, shop is open 24/7
- You can turn it on later in a save. If a girl already married her rival she stays married to him.
- Don't load a Marry all save in an older version of the mod, it breaks the game and the ladies disappear. 

### Title screen
- The Back to Nature logo on the title screen now has a +.

### Villagers page
- 5 villagers per screen with more room for the heads and the list wraps around from the bottom to the top.

### Fixes
- Expand Field: Barley's dog meeting and a few other big scenes could garble the trough area or freeze the game on a fully built farm. The trough now disappears while the scene runs and is back once you leave the area.
- Expand Field: the new field's squares now look right when tilled, watered and planted.
- Basic tool swings trails keep their own colour instead of the outfit's colour, except the basic watering can refill that still changes.
- The farmer keeps his outfit colour in event poses and at the music festival.
- Villagers page: a girl now shows her first heart when her heart turns purple.
- Villagers page: the heads could garble the start menu on the Goddess festival morning.


## New in v1.4

### Ocarina
- A new tool from **Pastor Carter**. He gives you the ocarina once you've **played at the Music Festival** in fall and he's at **full hearts**. 
- When played every animal around you counts as talked to and brushed for the day. Cows, sheep, hens, the dog and the horse all show their heart. It works in the barn, the coop and outside.
- The tune is the ocarina from the game's opening, played on the game's own ocarina sound. The playing animation is the one from the music festival.

### Villagers page
- **Options > Villager list: On or Off.** Off by default. Adds a **Villagers** page to the start menu. Everyone in town is listed A-Z with their head and their friendship in pink hearts, the same hearts your animals use. The girls hearts follow their affection.
- The heads are cut from each villager's own sprite in the game.

### Outfits
- Two new outfit colours at the blacksmith: Orange and Dark green. The outfit list now turns pages like the recipe list.
 
### Options
- The Options menu turns pages like the recipe list, with the game's orange arrows. L1 and R1 jump a page.

### Fixes
- Brushing cows and sheep now raises their hearts. In the original the brush did nothing for their hearts.


## New in v1.3

### Shipping report
- **Options > Shipping report: On or Off.** Off by default. When you go to bed it shows what you shipped that day, with pictures, the amount shipped, gold and the total gold. It goes up to 8 pages and the rest becomes others. 

### Shipping bin
- **Options > Shipping bin: Classic or Ship all.** A permanent choice for that save, like Rucksack. Ship all lets you ship your whole rucksack at a bin or from the horse's saddlebag from the start, without Gotz's upgrade. Classic keeps the original bin and Gotz still offers the upgrade.

### Options
- The Options window has a new **Back to nature+** section with all the mod's settings in one list. Left and right change the settings.

### Hard seasons
Four challenges that make the game harder. They're in **Options > Hard seasons**, all off by default. Once you turn them on they stay on for that save. The setting asks you first.  
- **Tools in order:** No skipping ore tiers at Saibara's. Copper, silver, gold, mystrile and then adamantite. 
- **Expensive town:** Your money drops to 0G when you turn it on and everything in the village costs 50% more. This includes the shops, seeds, animals, Saibara, Gotz, the clinic, the Inn and Won. The lumber for Gotz's jobs stays the same, and TV Shopping keeps its original prices.
- **Doctor's bill:** collapsing costs 2000G in year 1, 4000G in year 2 and 8000G from year 3 onwards.
- **Breeding livestock:** Lillia and Barley sell you one of each animal. The rest you breed or hatch. They sell again once you don't own any of that stock.

### Other
- The supermarket sends you a letter when the Rucksack+ is in, like it does for the large rucksack.
- Eggs sell by quality: normal 50G, good 60G, excellent 80G, golden 150G

### Fixes
- The Rucksack+ gave you about 32000G instead of charging 33333G. Now it charges you.

## New in v1.2

### Walk through crops
- Options > Crop walk: On or Off. Off by default, change it any time. When it's on you can walk through your planted crops, now you can harvest that middle crop all the time. 

### Stamina and fatigue under the clock
- Options > Stamina bar: **Bars**, **Numbers** or **Off**. Off by default, change it any time.
  - **Bars:** a green stamina bar (turns yellow, orange and red as you tire) and a blue fatigue bar (orange from 50, red from 80).
  - **Numbers:** "S 140/200" for stamina and "F 10" for fatigue, in small numbers and letters. A line turns red in its last 10%.
- It hides while menus and money counters are open.

### Fixes
- Fixed an item sometimes turning invisible in the rucksack and holding nothing when taking it out. Caused by the yarn ball in the tool bag and a full rucksack.
- The Up button now moves up in the Options menu, this was the games original setting, you could only go down.

## New in v1.1

### Rucksack stacks

- Open the **Options** window, its located at the bookshelf of your house. Pick **Rucksack**. It's a permanent choice for that save:
  - **Classic:** the original bag.
  - **Stacks:** 9 of a kind in a slot. Once you own the biggest rucksack, the supermarket sells the **Rucksack+** for 33333G, which raises it to 99.
  - **Stacks 99:** 99 of a kind in a slot from the start (no Rucksack+).

The entry disappears once you've chosen. Old saves also get a choice when you open options.
- What stacks: things of the same kind and quality. Crops, flowers, weeds, dishes, eggs, milk, wool, fish, ores, fodder. 
- Your hands always hold one item. **R2** with empty hands takes one from the first stack in the bag, if you put it back, it merges again and that stack moves to the end of the bag. This feels intuitive and works well for the game. Counts show on the menu screen, fridge, cabinet and kitchen. 
**Select** on a bag slot throws a whole stack away, it will ask you first. 
- Bulk shipping ships whole stacks. Events that take things from you (Ann's eggs and the mayors requests) take them from your stacks in any order.

### Text speed
- **Options > Text speed: Normal or Fast.** Fast makes the text about 6 times faster than the original while you hold x. Every box still waits for you to press X again, nothing is skipped.

### Farm score
- The farm score on the summary page can now reach **100** the way the game intended. Each Power Berry you eat counts 1 (there are 10) and the Mystic Berry counts as 2. In the original the berries were never added, so the score stopped at 88. In v1.0, I had made Adamantite/outfit bonus reach 100%; the score is the game's own formula again.
- If you only want this fix see **Farm score fix** below.

## What's new

### Tools
- **Adamantite tier**, above Mythril. Order it from Saibara like the other upgrades.
  - Bigger work areas: hoe 3x6, sickle 7x7, hammer 3x3.
  - The Adamantite can waters 36 squares.
  - The axe gives 18 lumber per stump and uses less stamina.
  - The hammer patches 3x3 tilled fields.
- Adamantite shows on the Level page and in the farm score.

### Gotz's farm upgrades
Each one costs lumber and gold, takes 3 nights to build and a letter arrives in your mailbox when it's done.
- **Bulk shipping:** empty hands + X at a shipping bin ships everything you carry. It also works when you're on the back of a grown horse and press triangle
- **Reinforce hothouse:** storms and blizzards can't destroy it.
- **Hardwood fences:** lumber you place never rots.
- **Automatic feeders:** troughs for animals inside are filled each night from your fodder stock.
- **Build bridge:** a log bridge to the lake island mine, spring to fall, gone in winter.
- **Expand field:** 190 extra squares east of the stable, plus a water trough by the stable.
- **Enlarge hothouse:** makes the hothouse much deeper, adding six more 3x3 plots to the original four.

### Animals
- **Miracle berry:** Won sells it for 10000G. Eat it and your animals stay happy in bad weather and stray dogs won't scare them.
- **Coloured hens:** when you buy a chicken from Lillia pick White, Brown, Lavender or Grey (same price).
  - Chicks grow up in the colour of a hen in your flock.
  - The colour shows everywhere: on the farm, when carried, in the start menu, in Lillia's sell list and in the chicken festival contest. Your rival in the contest is never your colour.
- Hens outside cluck once per visit instead of all the time.
- Best-herd and best-flock festival bonuses, four animals now get the gold badge if they meet the heart requirements. 

### Shopping and outfits
- **New outfits** at the blacksmith, 2000G each, with matching yarn balls: Red, Green, Purple, Black, Aqua, Pink, Yellow, Emerald, Teal and Dark blue, plus the original Overalls.
- **Running boots:** 7000G, help you sprint faster.
- Bulk food from the supermarket goes into the fridge.
- The cabinet is available earlier, the pile of boxes in the first house.

### Fixes to the original game
- Hothouse eggplant and similar items no longer show the wrong colours when carried overhead.
- The farm score can reach 100%. The original never added the power berries or the mystic berry to the score, so it stopped at 88%.
- Golden eggs sell for more than normal eggs, as their description always said. The original paid 50G for every egg.
- Casting the fishing rod at your horse put the lure in the saddlebag and counted it as a shipment, it didn't affect normal gameplay but caused a freezing issue with the shipping report.
- Brushing cows and sheep now raises their hearts (+2 each night you brush them). In the original the brush did nothing for their hearts even though its description says it does.
- Rivals stop pining for your wife. Rick, Kai, Gray and the Doctor no longer talk about winning over a girl who married you and Rick no longer keeps talking about Karen like she’s single once she’s your wife, in the original he got stuck at a certain romance line. 
- Popuri as your wife only says "Poor Karen" when Rick really married Karen.
- Fireworks Festival: Kai only turned up when Mary married Gray. He now comes when he marries Popuri. A girl married to her rival now answers the way she should.
- Fireworks Festival: Popuri asked you to watch the fireworks with her and then nothing happened. Her line no longer offers that choice.
- Proposing to a girl in the week before her rival's wedding cancelled the wrong couple's wedding and she could end up married to her rival as well as you. Very rare and hard to trigger in the original but with marry all you might race a rival to his wedding day. Now the right wedding is cancelled.

## How to install

You need your own copy of the game: Harvest Moon: Back to Nature (USA), SLUS-01115, as a .bin/.cue. It must be the clean, unmodified disc:

| | |
|---|---|
| CRC32 | `B0E8F078` |
| SHA-1 | `cb26381fa1336bdd15f07469e59f1143ccf37b03` |

A .chd of the USA disc works too. Turn it back into .bin/.cue first with `chdman extractcd`.

1. Back up your original .bin.
2. Get Delta Patcher (or any xdelta3 patcher).
3. Original file = your clean .bin. Patch file = `Harvest.Moon.Back.To.Nature.Plus.v1.6.xdelta`.
4. Apply. Point your .cue at the new .bin (rename the .bin to match, or edit the .cue's first line).
5. Play on an emulator (DuckStation, ePSXe, etc.) or burn it.

If the patcher says the source file doesn't match, your disc isn't the clean USA version.

Old saves load fine, but keep a copy of your memory card before playing, just in case something breaks.


## Farm score fix (stand-alone)

A separate one-change patch for people who want the **original game with only the farm score fixed**: Power berries count 1 each and the mystic berry 2, so a complete farm reads 100% instead of 88%. Nothing else is changed. It's applied to the clean disc the same way. Not needed with Back to nature+ v1.1, which already has the fix.
